Building at the Intersection of AI and Impact

I'm Lucky Nakola — a software engineer and AI enthusiast based in Nairobi, Kenya. I live at the intersection of technology and community impact, driven by the belief that great software can change lives.

My journey started with a fascination for how things work — from tinkering with code during my BTEC studies at Mpesa Foundation Academy to building AI-powered tools that solve real problems for businesses and communities.

Today, I specialize in full-stack web development and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on building solutions for the African context — education platforms, financial tools, healthcare systems, and community infrastructure.
